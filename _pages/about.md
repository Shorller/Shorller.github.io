I'm a data scientist and analyst with a background in statistics, public-sector analysis, sustainability research, and applied machine learning. My work focuses on using data to understand complex problems, communicate evidence clearly, and develop practical, data-driven solutions.

I currently work as a Customer Service Advisor and have recently completed a Data Science Internship at [Amdari.io](https://amdari.io), where I worked on applied machine learning projects. My projects span predictive modelling, classification, natural language processing, model explainability, and data visualisation.

Before moving into data science, I worked as a Statistician in the civil service and as a Research Intern on a sustainability project, gaining experience in sustainability and climate-related research. These experiences strengthened my interest in using quantitative evidence to support better decision-making across public-sector, environmental, and social contexts.

My academic background combines quantitative and social research. I hold a First Class BSc in Demography and Social Statistics, an MSc in Sociology (Merit), and an MSc in Earth Futures Research (Merit) from Obafemi Awolowo University, the University of Lagos, and the University of Glasgow respectively. My research on behaviour-led pathways to resilient urban waste systems was published in Environment and Urbanization.

I'm particularly interested in applying statistics, data science, and analytical skills to public-sector, environmental, sustainability, and social challenges. This portfolio brings together my data science projects, research, and practical applications of data analysis.

Feel free to explore my projects or get in touch if you'd like to talk about data, research, policy, or sustainability.
