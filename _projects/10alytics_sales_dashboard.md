A two-page Power BI dashboard built during a Power BI training programme, analysing company-wide sales performance alongside customer demographics and profitability. The dashboard is split into a **Sales** view and a **Demography** view, toggled via an in-report navigation bar.

### Sales performance

- **Total Sales: £470.53K** · **Total Profit: £61.62K** · **Profit Margin: 13.10%** across 2K total orders
- **Furniture led sales by category** (£0.17M), narrowly ahead of Technology (£0.16M) and Office Supplies (£0.14M)
- **Sales by region** showed the East as the strongest performer (31K), more than double the South (10K)
- **Sales trend across the year** showed a clear autumn peak, rising sharply from a June low (25K) to a September high (65K), before a dip and a strong November–December close

### Demography & profitability

- **573 total customers**, averaging **$821.20 in sales per customer**
- **Corporate was the most profitable customer segment** (47%+ of profit), ahead of Consumer (33%) and Home Office (19%)
- **Standard Class was the dominant shipping preference** (62% of orders), well ahead of First Class (20%)
- **New York and California drove the bulk of state-level profit**, with California generating the highest total sales (88K) despite New York's higher profit-to-sales efficiency
